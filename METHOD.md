# Evidence synthesis exercise

1. Write a specific topic and record the search date, journals, years and inclusion criteria.
2. Search titles and available abstracts. Save original DOI links; do not imply that a linked paper was read.
3. Read the accessible material and record the evidence level. Obtain full text only through authorized access.
4. Extract the main idea, setting, data, method, reported finding and reported limitation separately.
5. Compare findings across settings and designs. Mark your own interpretation as an inference.
6. State a tentative next question, a feasible data source and an identification strategy with assumptions.
7. Search for disconfirming and newer work. Keep novelty marked unverified until independently checked.
8. Draw a source-linked map: question → evidence → agreement/disagreement → limitations → proposed study.

The public app automates parts of this process. This exercise is intentionally manual and works without the private service or an AI subscription.
